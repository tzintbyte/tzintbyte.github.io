Privacy Policy

Stoic Trader by TzintByte LLC

Effective date: November 17, 2026

The short version: Stoic Trader does not collect, store, track or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information We Collect

None. Stoic Trader does not collect any personal information, usage analytics, location data, contacts or device identifiers. The app has no user accounts and does not require you to sign in.

Data You Enter in the App

The trades, journal entries and settings you enter (such as account size, risk percentage and trade slots) are stored only in the app's local storage on your iPhone or iPad. This data is never sent to TzintByte LLC or to any other server. We cannot see, access or recover it.

If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, that backup may include the app's data. Backups are managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by TzintByte LLC.

Tracking, Advertising and Third Parties

  • Stoic Trader contains no advertising.
  • Stoic Trader uses no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Stoic Trader does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data, because we do not collect any.

Network Access

Stoic Trader works fully offline. It does not connect to the internet to send or receive your data.

Information Handled by Apple

Your purchase and download of Stoic Trader are processed by Apple through the App Store. If you choose to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide TzintByte LLC with anonymous crash data. That information is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy and does not identify you.

Deleting Your Data

You are in full control of your data. You can erase all trades and journal entries from the app's Settings screen, or remove everything by deleting Stoic Trader from your device.

Children's Privacy

Stoic Trader does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Because we do not collect data, any change will be posted here rather than sent to you.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@tzintbyte.com.
