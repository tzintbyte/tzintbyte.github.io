Frequently Asked Questions

How does Stoic Trader calculate my position size?

In Buying Power mode, your account buying power (set in Settings) is divided by your number of trade slots, and the recommended shares are what that amount can buy at the ticker price.

In Dollar Risk mode, you enter the most you're willing to lose on the trade. Stoic Trader divides that amount by your risk per share (entry price minus stop price), and caps the result at what your per-trade buying power can afford.

Where do I set my account size, risk % and reward %?

Open the Settings tab. Your buying power, risk percentage, reward percentage and trade slots are saved there and used by the Calculator.

Why does the Calculator say "Capped by buying power"?

In Dollar Risk mode, the shares needed to reach your maximum dollar risk cost more than your per-trade buying power. Stoic Trader limits the shares to what you can afford, so your actual risk is lower than the amount you entered.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new device?

All trades and journal entries are stored only on your device. Stoic Trader does not sync to a server. When you move to a new iPhone or iPad, restoring from an iCloud or computer backup of your old device carries the app's data with it.

How do I edit or delete a trade?

In the trade list or a calendar day, swipe right on a trade to edit it or swipe left to delete it.

How do I reset all my data?

Go to the Settings tab and use the reset data option. This permanently erases your trades and journal entries from the device and cannot be undone.

I got a new device. How do I download Stoic Trader again?

Stoic Trader is a one-time purchase. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, search for Stoic Trader and download it again at no extra cost.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Stoic Trader give trading advice?

No. Stoic Trader is a calculation and record-keeping tool. It does not provide investment, financial or trading advice. Always make your own decisions and trade at your own risk.
